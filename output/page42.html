Kate Middleton Shares Never-Before-Seen Baby Photo of Son Prince Louis

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Princess Kate Middleton kicked off Halloween 2026 with a never-before-seen photo of her 7-year-old son, Prince Louis.

“Happy Halloween! 🎃,” the Princess of Wales wrote in a Saturday, October 31, celebratory post shared via her official Instagram account.

In the snap, Louis, then 12 months, wore a pint-size rain jacket and boots as he played with a round, orange pumpkin. Kate, 44, is credited with snapping the image.

Kate, who shares Louis with husband Prince William, famously considers herself to be an amateur photographer.

Revisit Kate Middleton’s 'Amateur’ Photography Portfolio

“I sort of learn on the way, but actually during this time [in lockdown], I spent lots of time picking up my camera and taking photos of the children because they’re always around us when we’re doing stuff together,” Kate explained of her passion on the U.K.’s This Morning in 2020. “One of the fantastic things about photography is really capturing that moment so it’s not staged.”

She continued at the time, “It’s not setting it up perfectly; it’s not clearing your house away so you got a perfect studio setup but it’s really capturing those moments that feel real to you.”

In addition to taking photos of her daily life, Kate has also photographed her children’s various birthday portraits over the years. (Kate and the Prince of Wales, 44, are also parents of son Prince George, 13, and daughter Princess Charlotte, 11.)

View this post on Instagram

“The Duchess of Cambridge is a very good photographer,” Kensington Palace curator Claudia Acott Williams exclusively told Us in 2022. “She has started releasing her own photographs of her children as a way of sharing their progress with the public. … By releasing her own photographs, she’s kind of maintaining an element of control and privacy for them.”

Per Williams, Kate’s portraits offer a unique “mother’s view” to typically formal snapshots.

“That’s an intimacy that we don’t often get,” Williams noted. “It creates a unique perspective. It is very clever. It really allows the public in a very intimate way, while still allowing them to maintain the privacy of family life.”

Everything Kate Middleton Has Said About 'Amateur' Photography Passion

Kate and William have tried to allow their children to have a sense of normalcy growing up. George recently matriculated at Eton College, a prestigious British boarding school that his father and uncle Prince Harry each attended.

“She said she’s looking forward to George coming home [and that] she’s missed him,” local patron Denise Ashton told Hello! after meeting Kate during her October 22 appearance at East Anglia’s Children’s Hospices in Ipswich, England. “They may be going somewhere [during the break].”

While speaking with another attendee, Kate noted that her children were anticipating their school break. She added, “I can’t wait to have them all home.”

Paul Merson makes Arsenal Premier League title claim ahead of Liverpool clash

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →
Former Arsenal midfielder Paul Merson (Picture: Sky Sports)

Paul Merson believes Arsenal can have the Premier League wrapped up by mid-December if they navigate a tricky run of fixtures that begins with a trip to Anfield on Sunday.

The Gunners moved level with Manchester City at the top of the table last weekend after a routine 2-0 home win against Everton.

But Mikel Arteta’s side were brought crashing back down to earth in midweek, suffering a penalty shootout defeat to League Two side Fleetwood Town in the Carabao Cup.

Looking to put that shock defeat behind them and get back to winning ways, Arsenal face a difficult trip to Merseyside to take on a Liverpool side that have won their last five matches in all competitions.

Sunday’s fixture marks the start of a tricky run of games for Arsenal, with games against Newcastle United, Manchester City and Tottenham all to come after the international break.

But Merson believes a positive string of results in those tricky games will put his former side in the driving seat to retain their title come the end of the season.

Speaking to Sky Sports, the former Arsenal midfielder said: ‘What a November Arsenal have, by the way. They’ve got a hard month, including the start of December.

  • November 7: Hull City (H)
  • November 21: Newcastle (A)
  • November 29: Man City (H)
  • December 2: Brentford (A)

‘You’ll know by December 13 if Arsenal will win the Premier League. Because there’s a saying: after the hard games, come the easy games.

‘If Bukayo Saka is injured for this period, he’s going to be missed. People say he isn’t playing well, I think he’s a decision-maker – he constantly makes those right decisions week in, week out.

‘Now there’s Noni Madueke and Eberechi Eze coming in and playing in his place.

‘It’s all well and good them coming in and playing for a couple, then sitting out for a couple – but when you’re asking them to play six to eight weeks on the trot, that’s a real tester.’

Conversely, Merson believes that Liverpool will have to get a positive result at Anfield on Sunday if they are to be seen as credible title challengers to Arsenal this season.

‘Liverpool are starting to cook a little bit, but they have got to win this Sunday if they want to be in the Premier League title race,’ he added.

‘If they don’t win, they stay six points behind at least and still have to go to the Emirates.

‘You’re then asking Arsenal to lose two football matches without you losing one. And given Liverpool will lose more games this season, you’re then asking Arsenal to lose three or four games for the rest of the season. That’s a lot of games.’

HBO teases a new season of It: Welcome to Derry with 30-minute Halloween special

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

After the success of HBO's horror TV show It: Welcome to Derry, released one year ago this month, it was no surprise that the network would renew the show for a second season, but nobody predicted that it would launch with a surprise special episode, released right on time for Halloween.

"A Story of Derry" is a 30-minute bonus episode that simultaneously points fans to the season ahead and takes them back in time to the very origins of the entity that would become Pennywise the Clown. 

SEE ALSO: 'IT: Welcome to Derry's chaotic ending, explained

Two YouTube/TikTok shorts were used to announce the special episode, the first more of a tease than the second, but both give us insight into the likely timeline of the series: sometime in the distant past, before the American founding. 

The episode itself follows three children venturing alone into the Western Wood, where they encounter not only a malevolent, terrifying spirit but also something cosmic and beautiful, a counterweight to the horror and violence of Pennywise.

Fans of the series will love this mini-episode, but they will also surely be disappointed because the second season of It: Welcome to Derry isn't in production yet and therefore has no release window, let alone a release date. In a recent interview with the New York Post, showrunner Brad Caleb Kane promised that the new season would exceed the terror and suspense of the last, and indicated that they're actively writing it now, so a late-2027 or early-2028 release date is still a possibility.

In the meantime, horror fans should definitely check out "A Story of Derry," not only for its own merits but for the additional backstory it brings to the Pennywise lore.

Bill Belichick and Jordon Hudson's Silent Split Surprised UNC Staff: Report

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Bill Belichick and Jordon Hudson kept their reported breakup so quiet, that not all of his University of North Carolina – Chapel Hill colleagues were aware of their updated relationship status.

Two staffers inside the UNC football office anonymously confirmed to USA Today Sports on Friday, October 30, that they were “not aware” Belichick, 74, and Hudson, 25, had called it quits. (Us has reached out to UNC, Belichick and Hudson for comment.)

Multiple outlets, including TMZ and People, reported earlier on Friday that the pair had privately parted ways last year but remained friendly. Hudson has even been a fixture at UNC home games and Belichick’s scouting trips this fall in the wake of their silent breakup.

Neither the Super Bowl-winning coach nor Hudson have publicly addressed the end of their romance. The duo had been together since 2021, often shutting down criticism about their 49-year age difference.

Bill Belichick and Jordon Hudson Quietly Split Nearly 1 Year Ago: Report

“Let’s take a deeper dive into the hot topic of the day: LOVE,” Hudson began a February 2025 post via Instagram. “‘What constitutes love?’ or, ‘What makes someone worthy of loving?’ are questions that require a deep analysis and could possibly enable one to understand the romances that they may not understand nor relate to.”

She continued, “Love does not look the same every time you see it. People and relationships, like everything else, are impermanent; even though love is eternal. Love is not to be judged. It is never OK to harass or abuse a person based upon who they love.”

Belichick was hired to coach the UNC football team in 2024, as his romance with the adult cheerleader often raised eyebrows for her presumed participation in his continued career.

UNC’s Bill Belichick Calls Out ‘Flat-Out Lie’ About Girlfriend Jordon Hudson

“When I took the job here, it was December, and at that time I was doing five TV shows, the radio show, I had a book coming out,” Belichick told the Wall Street Journal in September, downplaying Hudson’s involvement in his career. “I had a lot of other things going on when I took the job. I sent an email when I got here and I said, ‘Can you please forward any requests to Jordon about my personal appearances so that I can work and coordinate them outside the university?’”

He added at the time, “Didn’t have anything to do with the university. Well, that was leaked [and] released as ‘Jordon’s involved in every single thing that I do and she’s copied on every email.’ That’s just a flat-out lie.”

Belichick repeatedly stressed that Hudson, whom he has called a successful businesswoman, has only helped with his personal obligations outside of the gridiron.

“I stay focused on football and, some of the other opportunities that come along, Jordon looks at those and can dig into them a little bit deeper,” he said on the “Pivot” podcast last year. “But really, what she does is help me spend my time on football, and that’s what’s important to me.”
