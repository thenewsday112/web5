Two Honda Prelude Builds Show What a Type R Might Have Been

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

The Type R That Never Was

Some enthusiasts were not impressed with the Honda Prelude because its sporty styling and historic nameplate raised performance expectations, but its hybrid powertrain doesn’t necessarily meet them. The Prelude is powered by a 2.0-liter naturally aspirated inline-four with a two-motor hybrid system, delivering a combined output of 200 horsepower, slightly less than the CR-V Hybrid on paper.

However, enthusiasts may get what they want at the 2026 SEMA Show, where at least two custom Preludes will be showcased. Both feature Civic Type R powertrains, including a 2.0-liter turbocharged K20C1 engine that produces 315 horsepower in factory form and a 6-speed manual transmission. One was built by Hasport, a manufacturer of Honda and Acura engine-swap components, and will be displayed at the Honda Racing Corporation booth. The other comes from aftermarket tuning company throtl.

Honda

A Double Dose of Type R

The Hasport Prelude Club Racer is a track-focused build designed to compete in time attack events. Sporting a livery that pays tribute to the Hasport Prelude Circuit Monster, the custom car features a Borla exhaust system and suspension tuning derived from the Acura Integra Type S TCX race car.

On the other hand, throtl’s build is described as the “Prelude Type R that Honda never did.” This gives enthusiasts a reimagined version of what the Prelude could have looked like if it had come from the factory with a more potent powertrain. Well, Honda designed the production Prelude as a modern grand touring sports coupe that balances driving enjoyment, fuel efficiency, and everyday usability.

The aftermarket tuning company’s build is documented on its YouTube channel, which shows that the engine swap was far from straightforward, despite the Prelude and Civic sharing core underpinnings. There were several challenges, including modifying the front and rear bumpers and fabricating custom motor mounts.

Honda

Honda’s Racing Pedigree on Full Display

Aside from the Hasport Prelude Club Racer, the Honda Racing Corporation booth will showcase other performance vehicles at the upcoming SEMA Show, including the Civic Type R HRC Concept making its North American debut, 2027 Passport TrailSport Baja Chase, Honda-powered 2026 Aston Martin Aramco Formula 1 AMR26, and 2026 Indy 500-winning Honda IndyCar. HRC US will also introduce its first lineup of performance parts for both on-road and off-road vehicles.

The 2026 SEMA Show will run from November 3 to 6 at the Las Vegas Convention Center in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Game Preview #7: Wolves vs Spurs Game Thread

For the second time this week, the Wolves fly from Minnesota to take on a rested team on the tail end of a back-to-back. This is their fifth game in seven nights. They are down their A1 player in Anthony Edwards but have found a way to win without him. To add to their win total in San Antonio will take a full team effort with what they have left.

Game Info

  • Who:Minnesota Timberwolves (4-2) vs. San Antonio Spurs (3-2)
  • When: Saturday, October 31st at 7:00 PM CT
  • Where: Target Center – Minneapolis, MN
  • Local TV: DAZN-MN, DAZN-SAS
  • Radio: 830 WCCO

Conference Semi-Finals Rematch

This is the first matchup between the Wolves and Spurs since their playoff bout this past May. The Spurs would continue past Minnesota on their march to the final round where they were summarily spanked by the New York Knicks (thanks Karl).

Karl-Anthony Towns L of New York Knicks vies with Victor Wembanyama of San Antonio Spurs during the final game 5 between New York Knicks and San Antonio Spurs at the 2025-2026 NBA basketball game in San Antonio, the United States, June 13, 2026. (Photo by Wu Xiaoling/Xinhua via Getty Images)

The Wolves President of Basketball Operations Tim Connelly saw that the path through the Western Conference now curved through either Oklahoma City or San Antonio and their brand of swarming, hyper-athletic, in your jersey, perimeter defense. The Spurs being anchored by an All-Defense big man near the hoop.

As he did when building the Wolves to take on Nikola Jokic and the Denver Nuggets, he made a big trade that sent out precious draft capital and beloved players. The new vision was built around LaMelo Ball. An All-Star pass first point guard who can bend defenses in the half-court with either his passing or shooting.

In the past the Spurs tore up the hobbled Wolves by sending two defenders at Anthony Edwards who had little means of releasing the pressure in ways that punctured the defense. This left Victor Wembanyama clear to sit back and ward off the paint. The Wolves size that they had used to stifle the Nuggets was chop blocked.

Unfortunately we will not get to see Connelly’s full grand vision in action take on one of the very teams he built it for. As Edwards recovers from his successful arthroscopic surgery, the Wolves will instead have to contend with a team off to their own rocky start.

The Spurs are 3-2 with victories over the Dallas Mavericks without Cooper Flagg, the Sacramento Kings, and the Utah Jazz in overtime. At this writing they are middle of the league in shooting the basketball (tied at 18th in true shooting percentage at 56.2%), while boasting a top ten defense.

The Wolves have played well thus far against teams that lack consistent three point threats. Should the Spurs find some of their post-season form and hit from outside, it could get ugly fast.

Double Vision

San Antonio could employ the same defense they used in the playoffs against the Antless Wolves. Melo is a more capable ball handler and passer than Edwards and is better able to diagnose where to deliver the ball. What he is not, is someone who can handle a full night of physical bullying. Especially after playing a game 24 hours earlier.

Sending two players in any combination of Stephon Castle, Dylan Harper, or Devin Vassell could rattle Ball’s cage enough to turn the Wolves over. I expect the Spurs will try to rough up Melo and frustrate him. If Rudy tries to screen him open, Wemby will hedge just enough to prevent the deep shot knowing he can catch up to anyone in the play diving to the rim.

The rest of the Wolves will need to move well off the ball and keep the Spurs guessing where and when Melo might look to release the defensive pressure.

Centre Bleu!

Combing the standing reach of Rudy Gobert (9’7”), Joan Beringer (9’3”), and Wemby (somewhere between 9’7”-9’10”) we could run nearly one third of court tip to toe with French center. Outside of Luke Kornet minutes, the entirety of this game figures to see two of those three on the court at all times protecting the rim.

Where Wemby has more outside game than either Rudy or Joan, all of them do their best work on offense closer to the basket. Thankfully Rudy got back in the scoring column against the Clippers. As the elder homme d’État, he’ll need to bide his time to score against Wemby, collecting offensive rebounds when Big Vic is pulled far enough away from the rim and slamming them home before the defense can recover.

As the reigning Defensive Player of the Year, Wembanyama stalks around the basket like Jack Skellington on the hunt for a new holiday. What the Wolves, specifically Terrance Shannon Jr., discovered last year in the post-season, is that you have to go at the man in the middle. This will not always reap rewards, but if Minnesota can get past their perimeter defender they need to go at Victor and force him to make a play.

The more the Wolves can make Wemby exert himself on one end, the more he will lack on the other end by the fourth quarter. On the second night of a back-to-back, it is a big ask for Minnesota, but Wemby is at his worst in the fourth.

Prediction

The Wolves who battled the Spurs last year will come out looking to prove something. Ball and Jonathan Kuminga will feed off that energy and look to help their teammates looking to steal back their pride. Minnesota’s outside shots fall and they take the lead weathering a few surges by the Spurs. It gets close in the third but by the fourth, Wemby is spent and the Wolves tuck San Antonio into bed early, leaving a night light on for fear of the big bad wolf.

Manchester United director of football Jason Wilcox and chief executive Omar Berrada were spotted making an unusual move following the club’s latest Premier League defeat to Chelsea at Stamford Bridge.

According to video footage shared by The Sun, both Wilcox and Berrada were filmed boarding the Manchester United team bus alongside the playing squad following the match.

The action is particularly unusual because senior board executives typically travel back to Manchester separately or via private transport rather than riding on the team coach with the players and coaching staff.

The decision for the top hierarchy to join the squad directly on the bus has raised eyebrows across the footballing world, reflecting the immense gravity of the club’s ongoing crisis and signaling that serious internal discussions are taking place behind closed doors.

Michael Carrick facing growing pressure after Chelsea defeat

The unusual moment comes with head coach Michael Carrick under overwhelming pressure as United’s horrid form continues.

Following the 2-0 defeat in West London, which featured a red card for Lisandro Martínez and goals from Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer, traveling supporters at Stamford Bridge once again made their voices heard.

While fans applauded Carrick and the players, vocal chants were aimed directly at Wilcox and Berrada from the away end, criticizing the club’s recruitment and summer transfer strategy.

With United currently undergoing their longest winless streak since 1988,  and facing a crucial UEFA Champions League clash against Roma on Tuesday, Carrick is under immense pressure to deliver.
