Dozens of Unexplained Drones Have Buzzed America's Nuclear Plants Since 2024

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →
Slashdot reader ElectricVs shares this report from American Buildout, a new site covering "the intersection of data centers, energy and power, finance, and Main Street." Dozens of unexplained drones flew over and near U.S. nuclear facilities between February 2024 and March 2026, according to records the Nuclear Regulatory Commission made public this week. In all, facility operators filed 67 reports of drones over or near 28 sites in 17 states. They include the Three Mile Island site near Middletown, Pennsylvania., where Constellation Energy plans to restart Unit 1 as the Crane Clean Energy Center. In one incident, about five drones flew roughly 100 feet above the [970-megawatt] reactor building at River Bend Station in Louisiana. The story was first reported by the publication The Liberation Times. The NRC published the documents on its website in response to the publication's freedom of information request... Every entry carries the status "Closed Unresolved." "Most of the incidents happened after dark," the article points out, "and none of the records identifies a drone operator. About half of the incidents, 33 of 67, occurred in an eight-week window between Dec. 3, 2024, and Jan. 24, 2025." (Virginia's Surry Power Station experienced five different incidents in December of 2024, including one involving "at least eight drones in two waves.") 100 miles west of Chicago, "drone activity" continued for more than two hours at the Byron Nuclear Station, where two separate drones were reported. In Waterford, Connecticut, "security observed four drones flying back and forth..." The article notes a 2024 paper from the Nuclear Regulatory Commission citing a 2019 analysis that "concluded U.S. plants had no risk-significant vulnerabilities that adversaries could exploit with commercially available drones to cause radiological sabotage, or theft or diversion of special nuclear material."

West Ham fans turn on Nuno Espirito Santo amid more Championship misery

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

West Ham fans were heard chanting ‘Sacked in the morning’ to boss Nuno Espirito Santo during their 3-1 defeat to West Brom.

The Hammers have dropped to eighth in the Championship following a second consecutive defeat in England’s second-tier.

West Ham fans turned on boss Espirito Santo as they fell to defeat at home against West BromGetty

West Brom went ahead via Felix Horn Myhre with 20 minutes played, before Jarrod Bowen equalised with his seventh goal of the season.

But Jimmy-Jay Morgan and Callum Styles wrapped up the points for the visitors at the London Stadium to go fourth in the table.

Towards the end of the match, West Brom fans sang ‘Sacked in the morning’ to Espirito Santo, who used to manage their rivals Wolves.

They were joined by the home faithful, with West Ham supporters turning on their head coach for a second time this season.

Espirito Santo faced the same chant during a 1-1 draw with Watford in their third match of the season.

West Ham have now lost three of their seven home matches in the Championship this term.

What has been said about West Ham?

Speaking on talkSPORT commentary, Perry Groves stressed he doesn’t believe the Hammers will achieve automatic promotion back to the top-flight.

He said: “This West Ham, I wouldn’t say team now, a bunch of players.

“At the beginning of the season, you’d have said that they should be nailed on to being in the top two.

“You’re looking at this, and yet the evidence we’ve seen in front of our eyes, is you would say that they have got no chance of being in the top two if they carry on like this, because it’s just too disjointed.”

West Brom leapfrogged West Ham into fourth with their away victoryGetty

Groves later added: “The only reason I say that he’s not under pressure is maybe the West Ham hierarchy and how much it will cost to get rid of him, obviously, with the compensation payment.

“So maybe because of his record in the Championship with Wolverhampton Wanderers, it might give him a bit of a say.

“But if they don’t turn this around before the international break, that’s when decisions could be made.

“And West Ham are just a team of individuals, you know you have the problem at Manchester United where the Manchester United players… the culture stinks.

“This is going towards that end because there was no urgency at all.”

West Ham are now four points outside the top two in their bid to win instant promotion back to the Premier League.

Espirito Santo has called upon the West Ham fans to stick together amid their poor run of formGetty

They have two wins from their last six in the league and, speaking to talkSPORT after, Espirito Santo sent a message to the fans as he said: “We need to stick together.

“That’s the only solution to go back to results, sticking together, helping each other, improving our game. If we do this, the fans are going to be with us.”

XRP Reserves on Binance Slump to 2022 Lows — Is a Supply-Shock Rocket Surge Imminent?

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Could Ripple-linked XRP be sitting on the brink of a massive supply squeeze? Fresh on-chain data has revealed a striking development: XRP reserves on Binance have tumbled to their lowest level in nearly four years, with millions of tokens disappearing from the exchange’s holdings. The sudden drop is fueling speculation that a supply shock could be brewing, potentially setting the stage for an explosive price rally if investor demand returns.

XRP Vanishes From Binance as Exchange Reserves Hit 4-Year Lows 

While the broader crypto market continues to battle persistent selling pressure and weeks of volatility, XRP is flashing a potentially bullish on-chain signal. Despite struggling to gain upward momentum amid the downturn, the token is seeing its exchange reserves shrink, suggesting that an increasing amount of XRP is moving off trading platforms.

According to data shared by CryptoQuant analyst Amr Taha, XRP reserves on Binance plunged to 2.47 billion tokens on October 30, marking their lowest level since December 2022. 

Specifically, between October 28 and October 30, Binance’s XRP reserves fell from 2.69 billion to 2.47 billion tokens, representing a decrease of 220 million XRP, or approximately 8.18%, in just two days. The rapid drawdown has drawn attention to the remaining XRP on exchanges, raising questions about whether the trend could contribute to a potential supply squeeze if buying demand strengthens.

Per Taha, the decline in XRP reserves extended beyond Binance to other major exchanges. On Bybit, holdings fell from 306 million XRP to 286 million, a decrease of 20 million tokens. Meanwhile, Bithumb’s reserves slipped from 1.847 billion XRP to 1.837 billion XRP, marking a reduction of 10 million tokens.

Could XRP’s Supply Crunch Supercharge Its Price?

The decline in XRP reserves across multiple exchanges could reduce the supply readily available for immediate trading, potentially supporting a price recovery if buying demand strengthens. However, outflows alone do not confirm accumulation, as investors may simply be moving tokens to private wallets or other platforms.

For XRP bulls, the key question is whether declining exchange balances will coincide with a meaningful recovery in buying demand. If renewed investor interest meets a tighter supply of tokens available for sale, the combination could provide support for a strong price rally. Until then, the falling reserves remain an encouraging on-chain development for holders as XRP hovers around $1.25.
