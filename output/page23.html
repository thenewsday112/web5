Minute Review: American Airlines 737 MAX 8 First Class

American Airlines’ 737 MAX 8 First Class is not going to win awards for innovation, but on a recent flight from Dallas to Burbank, a comfortable seat and a fantastic Texas barbecue dinner made for a very pleasant journey and the subject of this week’s Minute Review.

American Airlines 737 MAX 8 First Class Minute Review (Video)

I’m continuing my series of 60-second flight reviews, and today we’re taking a quick look at American Airlines First Class on the Boeing 737 MAX 8.

There’s nothing particularly revolutionary about the Oasis and seat and many hate it, but at least it’s a standard domestic recliner with more legroom than economy class, power, storage, and individual air vents. For a flight of a few hours, though, it gets the job done just fine. I know some of you think this should not be called “first class” or even “business class” (since it is similar to an international premium economy seat), but AA markets this flight as first class so that’s what I’m calling it.

But the real reason I booked this particular flight was dinner.

I previously wrote about American Airlines’ new Pecan Lodge barbecue platter, which I deliberately routed through Dallas to try. It included smoked beef brisket, sausage, macaroni and cheese, coleslaw, pickles, onions, peppers, and barbecue sauce.

And it was excellent…I’m still salivating over it again today.

The brisket remained tender and juicy despite being reheated onboard, the sausage was flavorful, and even the macaroni and cheese came out wonderfully. This was a genuinely delicious barbecue dinner, not just decent airlines food.

By: Isak Möller

Ruben Amorim has named his starting eleven for tonight’s game between AC Milan and Inter, making six changes compared to the defeat against Bologna. 

After what was a disappointing mid-week display, the Rossoneri need to get back on track with a strong performance against Inter this evening. Thankfully, several key players are back at Amorim’s disposal, which should increase their chances.

AC Milan vs. Inter: Official starting XIs

Official Milan XI (4-3-3): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Pavlovic, Diawara; Musah, Modric, Rabiot; Moreira, Ramos, Pulisic.

Bench: Bouyer, Torriani; Bartesaghi, Estupiñan, Perera, F. Terracciano, Vladimirov; Cisse, Comotto, Hutchinson, Jashari, Vos; Camarda, Menon, Ossola.

Official Inter XI (3-5-1-1): Martinez; Bisseck, Akanji, Bastoni; Spence, Barella, Calhanoglu, Sucic, Carlos Augusto; Diouf; Thuram.

Bench: Di Gennaro, Taho; Bovio, Dimarco, Jakirović; Mkhitaryan, Stanković, Zieliński; Bonny, Esposito, Lavelli, Mosconi.

The returning players are Mario Gila, Adrien Rabiot and Diego Moreira, all three of whom are playing from the first minute. Fikayo Tomori’s injury means there was little choice in defence, while Rabiot is a given in games like these.

Moreira starting is perhaps not a surprise, but to see him on the right instead of the opposite flank raises doubts. He has performed at his best on the left, without a doubt, and the performances on the right have actually been quite poor.

Photo by Marco Luzzani/Getty Images

In addition to those three players, the likes of Sankhoun Diawara, Luka Modric and Christian Pulisic have been brought into the starting eleven. The youngster is the big surprise here as Davide Bartesaghi and Pervis Estupinan are available too.

A total of six changes, as such, with Amorim hoping that he can get a good response in front of the fans at San Siro.

As for Inter, there are quite a few surprises with the likes of Djed Spence and Petar Sucic starting, while Andy Diouf is playing up front instead of on the flank. Victor Provedel is out of the squad completely due to the flu.

30-Foot ‘Silver Dragon’ Tidal Bore Swallows Fisherman on China’s Qiantang River (Video)

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Mother Nature, and specifically the ocean, has a habit of reminding people who’s boss. And in China, apparently, it doesn’t even need a beach.

An alarming video circulating on Instagram shows a fisherman getting absolutely swallowed by a raging surge on the Qiantang River. The man is fishing when a wall of churning water bears down. Within moments, the tidal bore engulfs him, turning what appeared to be an ordinary outing into something resembling a disaster movie.

Check out the video, linked here, and below.

View this post on Instagram

The video provides no date or precise location along the river. There’s also no confirmed information about whether the fisherman was injured. What it does show, though, is the astonishing violence of a natural phenomenon known as the “Silver Dragon.”

The Qiantang River, which winds through eastern China before emptying into Hangzhou Bay, boasts the world’s largest tidal bore. It happens when the incoming ocean tide funnels into a narrowing, shallow estuary, piling up into a wave that barrels upstream against the river’s current.

Related: Watch: 130-feet Wide River Wave Just Erupted in Cabo

And this isn’t some ankle-high novelty. According to Guinness World Records, the bore can reach an extraordinary 30 feet and charge upstream at speeds approaching 25 mph (40 km/h). Essentially, it’s an enormous moving wall of water with very little interest in what’s standing in its way.

Naturally, surfers have tried to ride it.

In 2010, Jamie Sterling, Mikala Jones, Robert “Wingnut” Weaver, and Mary Osborne tackled the Qiantang bore. A few years later, Jamie O’Brien won the 2014 Red Bull Qiantang Shoot Out, a contest held on the muddy, unpredictable river wave.

“It’s definitely an obstacle course,” O’Brien told NPR, describing boats, piers, and concrete pilings scattered along the route. Unlike an ocean wave, the Silver Dragon can deliver rides lasting minutes rather than seconds.

But the fisherman in this video wasn’t chasing a record-setting ride. He appears to have found himself in the path of a wave that doesn’t take no for an answer.
