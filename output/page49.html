‘Act III’ of mass protests can’t run through central Paris on Halloween, France’s top court rules

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

PARIS (CN) — The Conseil d’État, France’s top court, ruled Saturday morning that “Act III” of the mass demonstrations could not pass through the city center, just hours before its planned kickoff at Place de la Bastille.

“There is a very high risk that… further major disturbances to public order will occur on [Saturday] on the fringes of the authorised processions, in a tense social and protest-related context, and at a time when numerous calls for violent actions on that day are being circulated on social media,” the court decision read. “The prohibition of this route and the proposal of an alternative … appears necessary and is neither inappropriate nor disproportionate.”

The new route, from Porte de Bagnolet to Porte de Charenton, runs along the outskirts of the capital. Saturday afternoon, buses heading toward the area were rerouted and nearby streets were closed to cars and bikes.

A protester holds a French flag at Republic Square during a demonstration against rising living costs, joining students’ grievances, in Paris, Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026. (AP Photo/Thibault Camus)

The ongoing protests are fueling debate over demonstrators’ fundamental rights and the state’s responsibility to maintain order. In Paris, the route of the demonstrations has become a political and philosophical flashpoint.

Protesting is a fundamental right under the French Constitution. Unions must declare their intended route in advance so authorities can prepare, but do not need permission to demonstrate because protests fall under freedom of expression. Authorities can impose restrictions only when there is a serious safety threat.

On Oct. 24, “Act II” of the demonstrations was pushed to the outskirts of Paris, where hundreds of thousands marched in the rain. This week, the interunion alliance declared that “Act III” would follow a traditional demonstration route from Place de la Bastille to Place de la Nation, used for similar marches for decades.

Paris police prefect Patrice Faure then announced that demonstrations along the route would be banned “for a while.” The prefecture said “Act III” would be allowed only along the city limits, citing Halloween as a public order risk because of potential masks, fake weapons and social media calls to emulate the American horror movie “The Purge.”

The interunion alliance, calling the decision an infringement on democratic rights, appealed to the Paris Administrative Court on Friday evening and won.

“The judge in chambers begins by recalling the scope of the freedom to demonstrate, which extends to the right to choose the time, place, and modalities of the gathering,” Chirinne Ardakani, a lawyer representing the interunion alliance, live-posted from the hearings. “The judge does not see how the imposed alternative route would avoid ‘clashes’ … alleged by a ‘vague and unsubstantiated’ note.’”

Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez announced the prefecture would immediately appeal to the Conseil d’État, France’s equivalent of the Supreme Court. Hearings began at 10 p.m. Friday and continued into early Saturday.

The court ultimately ruled in Nuñez’s favor, citing a heightened risk of public disorder, the Oct. 17 riots, threats to businesses along the Bastille-Nation route and demands on police resources. It concluded that the alternative route preserved the right to protest.

Roughly 70,000 officers were deployed nationwide Saturday.

Arsenal transfer news: Gunners target Brazilian teenager Eduardo Pape

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Arsenal are increasing their interest in Cruzeiro youngster Eduardo Pape.

Despite the fact he has yet to play for the senior side in Brazil, the 17-year-old has attracted interest from a number of clubs. It is understood that Arsenal is keeping an eye on the youngster. Newcastle United and Bayern Munich are also linked with the midfielder.

The move could be a good fit with the Gunners liking to identify young talent early on. However, Pape is still very much in the early stages of his career, and any move would be seen as an investment into his future.

Arsenal face competition for Cruzeiro prospect

Arsenal are reportedly targeting Pape as a potential signing in 2027. The teenager is valued at around £33m.

Pape has already had previous experience playing for Cruzeiro’s Under-20s. He’s played 32 times and scored three goals and three assists.

He has left a successful headline in junior football as well. Pape has netted six goals in eight matches and strives for a place in the Brazilian U17 World Cup squad.

The tournament gives an opportunity to demonstrate his skills. The scouts will closely monitor the tournament, so any team interested in the player will be able to see his progress.

Of course, not just Arsenal would want to sign him. Clubs like Newcastle and Bayern Munich have already expressed interest in securing him. This is likely to cause a competitive atmosphere.

Why Pape could suit Arsenal’s recruitment strategy

There is a concern that there hasn’t been any senior-level play from Pape. As he has never played first-team football before. It will be difficult to see how he will adapt to playing in Europe. 

The contract is until December 2029, meaning Cruzeiro are in a strong position. There isn’t a huge need for the Brazilian club to rush into any decisions, meaning Pape can get some experience in a comfortable environment before moving abroad.

However, they have been very successful in the past at bringing in young Brazilian players. Gabriel Martinelli was brought in from Ituano as a teenager and has become an important first team player.

It gives us an insight into what Pape could hope to achieve.

It shows that Arsenal are looking further ahead with their recruitment. Whether they make a move for him remains to be seen. Pape will be one to keep an eye on as we approach the 2027 transfer window.

Lisandro Martinez was ignored by Manchester United boss Michael Carrick after getting two yellow cards in 82 seconds against Chelsea.

Carrick refused to acknowledge Martinez on his way down the tunnelAFP

Cole Palmer dispatched the spot-kick to put the Blues 2-0 up, with Morgan Rogers having broken the deadlock early in the second half.

Rogers breezed past Martinez for the goal – and forced the Argentina defender into a clumsy challenge for his first yellow card.

It left Man United heading for yet another defeat amid already intense pressure on Carrick, who appeared to blank Martinez as he headed off down the tunnel.

Carrick was backed by the board ahead of the trip to London, but time is ticking on his time in the dugout.

Martinez in hot water again

The costly red card comes weeks after Martinez called out the United hierarchy in a bombshell interview.

Martinez’s dismissal came moments after talkSPORT’s Stuart Pearce had slammed his second-half display.

“Martinez has lost his head, he’s making some incredible rash decisions,” the legendary former England defender said.

Speaking after the red card, Pearce added: “I’m not sure I’ve seen a worse 20 minutes from a player.”

Is Martinez’s United career over?

Ex-Chelsea and England midfielder Joe Cole, commentating on the game for TNT Sports, went as far to suggest Martinez’s United days are numbered.

“Martinez, it’s run its course,” he said. “In a Manchester United shirt, he’s at the scene of the crime too often, so I think that’s probably his time done.”

Martinez inexplicably shoved Fofana off the ball

Martinez was also dismissed in a 2-1 home loss to Leeds back in April.

The 13-time Premier League champions have won just one of their opening nine league games this season, losing four.

Blues Earn Solid Point Against United

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Travel  |  Read Source →

Everton played out a goalless draw with Manchester United at Goodison Park on Saturday evening.

The Blues threatened throughout, with Yuka Momiki and Ornella Vignola both seeing strikes palmed away. At the other end, heroics from Courtney Brosnan kept the Toffees in it as they earned a solid point against the Red Devils on Halloween.

Brosnan and Clare Wheeler were presented with commemorative framed shirts prior to kick-off to mark the duo surpassing a century of appearances in royal blue.

Brosnan donned the captain’s armband in between the sticks, with a backline of Hikaru Kitagawa, Rion Ishikawa, Naomi Layzell and Hannah Blundell in front of her.

Ruby Mace and Wheeler sat deep in midfield, allowing Rosa van Gool – who scored in midweek against Sheffield United – to roam further forward.

Maz Pacheco started on the unfamiliar left-wing, with Momiki lining the opposite flank. Vignola shifted over to a central position to lead the line.

It was the hosts who threatened first as Momiki drifted inside and let fly from the edge of the box. Her effort had sufficient power, but whistled wide of the near-post.

The resilient Blues continued to show real spirit and battled hard throughout the opening 45, with neither side able to make the breakthrough.

Scott Phelan’s side emerged from the tunnel with an urgency about them – Vignola firing an effort in to the chest of Le Tissier from the edge of the box immediately after play resumed.

United reacted, with Brosnan called in to action to deny Dominique Janssen on 75 minutes. The Netherlands-international latched on to former-Blue Jess Park’s threaded through ball. With her first-touch, Janssen looked to catch the keeper off-guard, but Ireland’s number one was well-prepared.

Noemie Mouchon, who also netted against the Blades on Wednesday, replaced Momiki with nine minutes remaining as Phelan looked to his bench for a late goal.

After three minutes of injury time, the referee concluded the goalless affair to mark a solid point for the Toffees on home soil.

The Blues travel to face Chelsea in the capital next up, making the journey on Saturday 7 November (12pm KO).
