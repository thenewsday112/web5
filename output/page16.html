French Lawmakers Back Per-Gigabyte Internet Traffic Tax

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →
Companies like Google, Netflix, TikTok, and Meta may soon owe the French government, reports Tom's Hardware: France has approved a proposed tax that aims to tax the amount of internet data per gigabyte sent to French users in the country... According to minutes released by the National Assembly, members voted 11-10 in support of the motion on October 30 — which now means the new law has been added to the country's next budget, despite major opposition from the government side and the finance committee rapporteur. The latest amendment, introduced by socialist lawmaker Laurent Lhardit, could see content providers pay €0.055 (roughly $0.06) to the government for every gigabyte of content distributed to French users. With approximately 154 million users and counting in France, the proposal's authors say such a tax could generate around €10 billion ($11.2 billion) in annual revenue for the government. The said proposal, though yet to be passed as it must survive further parliamentary scrutiny, aims at companies that generate substantial internet traffic as opposed to taxing their advertising revenue, profits and subscriptions. Its explanatory notes particularly mention Google, Meta, Netflix, Amazon, and content delivery network Akamai as major potential contributors. Notably, TikTok and other similar platforms could fall under this broader scope depending on how these rules are applied. Internet service providers, including Orange Free and Liad, would be required to measure the volume of data and identify the companies responsible for sending it to French users. The Data will then be collected and submitted quarterly to France's tax administration under the supervision of telecommunications watchdog Arcep. The measure becomes law if it clears the remaining stages of France's budget process, the article points out, "with parliament scheduled to vote on the budget's first section in early November." "VideoLAN, the French nonprofit organization behind the VLC media player, warned that the proposed levy could cost it more than €1 million annually," reports CircleID. "Its response illustrates how traffic-based taxation could affect organizations distributing large volumes of data without corresponding commercial revenue."

Harry Kane in unwanted 14-year first as Borussia Dortmund stun Bayern Munich

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Harry Kane missed a penalty as Bayern Munich dropped points in the 2-2 Der Klassiker draw with Borussia Dortmund.

The newly-crowned Ballon d’Or winner smashed the woodwork with his effort from 12 yards early in the second half at the Allianz Arena with Bayern leading 1-0.

Kane became the first player since Robben in 2012 to miss a penalty during Der Klassiker between Bayern and DortmundGetty

Kane has only failed to convert a spot-kick 16 times during his career, whilst netting 113 of the total 129 he has taken.

However, the striker’s penalty miss on Saturday evening was the first in the Der Klassiker between Bayern and Dortmund since 2012.

Bayern legend Arjen Robben was the last player to squander such an opportunity during a 1-0 Bundesliga defeat in April 2012.

Bayern held to draw by Dortmund

Konrad Laimer put the hosts in front in the 33rd minute shortly after Dortmund had a Jobe Bellingham goal disallowed for a foul.

Serhou Guirassy levelled on the hour mark, only for Kane to make up for his penalty miss by assisting Dayot Upamecano.

He headed the ball across the face of goal for the defender to pick up an eighth assist of the campaign.

Dortmund were close to their first defeat of the season, but saved by Fabio Silva with a minute to go thanks to his well-taken shot.

The historic rivals are level on 18 points with five wins and three draws, although Bayern are top of the Bundesliga on goal difference.

Bayern have lost only one of their last 18 competitive meetings with Dortmund, winning 13 and drawing four.

They are also unbeaten in their last 22 Bundesliga matches as leaders against the second-placed side, the league’s longest-ever run.

Kane had a helping hand in one of Bayern’s goals, but they could not hold onto their lead and had to settle for a draw in the derbyGetty

Before kick-off, Kane was given a hero’s welcome by Bayern fans as they celebrated his Ballon d’Or win.

The England captain walked out onto the pitch and showcased the iconic golden ball to the crowd.

“I’m extremely proud to be a Bayern player,” he said. “I hope we win many more titles together.”

It was revealed on Friday that Kane won the 2026 Ballon d’Or ahead of Kylian Mbappe by just 28 points.

This is the fourth-closest voting gap in the prestigious award’s 70-year history.

Kane is now the fifth Englishman to win the award after Sir Stanley Matthews, Sir Bobby Charlton, Kevin Keegan and Michael Owen.
