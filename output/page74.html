‘Not happening’: Andy Hinchcliffe unconvinced by Stoke City summer signing

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Andy Hinchcliffe has admitted he has been underwhelmed by George Hirst at Stoke City since his move from Ipswich Town.

Stoke City landed the striker in the summer transfer window and handed him a three-year deal at the Bet365 Stadium. SCFC forked out a hefty £10million price tag to lure the forward to Staffordshire back in August.

Hirst, 27, has struggled for form so far for the Potters though and hasn’t made an immediate impact for Mark Robins’ side. He has made 12 appearances in all competitions this season, 11 of which have come in the Championship, and he has chipped in with one goal and one assist.

Photo by Mat Fascione, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.0.

Hinchcliffe, who played for Manchester City, Everton and Sheffield Wednesday, has delivered his honest verdict on the player, as per Sky Sports: “George Hirst is one player who hasn’t really got going yet. He’s one signing who hasn’t hit the ground running, and he’s not starting many games. I expected Hirst to go straight into the team and stay there, playing every game, but that’s not happening. He’s someone who could come to the fore a bit more as well.”

Stoke City drew 0-0 at home against Bristol City last time out. They are back in action this weekend on their own patch again against Preston North End.

Stoke City slow start for summer signing George Hirst

Stoke City paid big money to snap up Hirst from Ipswich Town. Therefore, he is under pressure to perform at SCFC.

The Potters acquired him to bolster their attacking department. He provides them with more competition and depth at the top end of the pitch.

Hirst, who is from Sheffield, battles it out with the likes of Lamine Cisse, Robert Bozenik and Sam Gallagher for a place in Robins’ starting XI. As Hinchcliffe has alluded to, he isn’t a guaranteed starter at the moment.

The Scotland international fired 23 goals in 102 outings for Ipswich Town during his time at Portman Road and helped the Tractor Boys gain promotion to the Premier League twice. He has also had spells in the past at Sheffield Wednesday, OH Leuven and Leicester City, among others.

The Verdict: George Hirst can still come good for Stoke City over the coming months

It has taken Hirst a while to adapt to life at Stoke City which can happen. SCFC need him to hit form soon though if they are to establish themselves as serious promotion contenders.

The Potters have been strong this term to date and are just outsidr the play-offs after 12 fixtures. They are outside the top eight on goal difference behind Southampton, Birmingham City and QPR.

Robins’ side were left frustrated after they failed to score against Bristol City in their last outing. They will be eager to return to winning ways against Preston North End and could do with Hirst finding the net against the struggling Lilywhites.
