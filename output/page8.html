Shigeru Miyamoto Proclaims November 1 is Pikmin Day, Pikmin 4 Demo Coming To Switch 2 Soon

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

This morning, Nintendo shared some updates related to the Pikmin franchise and has officially claimed November 1 as Pikmin Day, starting today and moving forward presumably forever.

 

Aside from the announcement of Pikmin Day as a general idea, Pikmin creator Shigeru Miyamoto appeared on camera to remind everyone that Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy is coming November 12. A free demo for the game, which will include one standard level and two Dandori Academy levels, will be available on November 2.

The Switch 2 version of the game (you can read Game Informer’s Pikmin 4 review here) will have improved visuals and take advantage of the Switch 2’s mic to let you say things like “Rush” and “Go to work” to Oatchi. The Dandori Academy side of the game is essentially time trials you can practice, and then on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, you will have one chance to post your best time. You can read more about the Switch 2 version of the game here.

Miyamoto also shared that the mobile game, Pikmin Bloom, is also getting a fairly substantial update soon with secret caves. In Bloom, players will be able to find maps that reveal caves that you can send your Pikmin into to battle and find treasure. You can also find maps for Bloom by playing Pikmin 4. Caves will be coming to Pikmin Bloom later this year.

The Pikmin Day presentation also reminded viewers that every Pikmin game is available on Switch and Switch 2, though no announcements or hints were made implying that Pikmin 1 and 2 would be coming to the Nintendo Switch Online subscription service, which includes GameCube games on Switch 2. Those games can be purchased individually on the eShop.

Finally, Miyamoto brought actress Fuka Koshiba on the presentation (best known for voicing Kiki in Kiki’s Delivery Service) to share her appreciation for the Pikmin franchise (she likes to knit Pikmin hats that she shares on social media) and introduce a Pikmin short film. In the short, a Pikmin interacts with a toad, but I won’t spoil what happens. Miyamoto promised more shorts are on the way and in the future, they will be found in the Nintendo Today app.

Unfortunately, the stream concluded without a single reference to 2017's Hey! Pikmin. I wouldn't expect it to get a port to Switch 2 anytime soon.

Of eight use of helmet infractions in Week 7, only Cam Skattebo was flagged

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

The fine imposed on Giants running back Cam Skattebo for illegal use of the helmet was not a rarity. The flag thrown in real time for it was.

Of eight fines levied by the NFL for use-of-helmet violations in Week 7, only Skattebo's came with a flag.

The league fined Skattebo $7,073. The seven players fined but not flagged were: Bears cornerback Clark Phillips ($11,550); Broncos safety Brandon Jones ($23,882); Broncos Alex Singleton ($47,662); Lions safety Brian Branch ($23,882); Colts cornerback Cam Taylor-Britt ($7,791); Steelers receiver Brandon Smith ($4,916); and 49ers running back Jordan James ($6,194).

The fines increase as the violations repeat. For Singleton, who was fined $23,882 for illegal use of the helmet, it was the second straight week he was fined.

The officials, for whatever reason, don't often call penalties for illegal use of the helmet. That's what made the penalty called on Skattebo so unusual.

The fact that the hit flattened Texans safety M.J. Stewart likely was a factor in the decision to penalize Skattebo. Still, the fact that only one of eight instances resulted in a foul being called underscores the fact that, when it comes to the rule against lowering the helmet and initiating forcible contact against an opponent, it's something that costs players money far more routinely than it costs their teams field position.

Netflix’s New Crime Docuseries Is Already Its No. 1 Show in America

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Netflix has a new No. 1 show, and it’s a three-part documentary about one of the most notorious figures in the history of the Sinaloa Cartel.

The Fall of El Mayo is currently the No. 1 series on Netflix in the U.S., according to FlixPatrol’s Oct. 31 rankings. The documentary arrived on the streaming service just two days earlier, on Oct. 29.

The series tells the story of Ismael “El Mayo” Zambada García, the co-founder of the Sinaloa Cartel and longtime partner of Joaquín “El Chapo” Guzmán. Unlike El Chapo, who became one of the most recognizable faces of the drug trade, El Mayo spent decades operating largely out of the public eye.

That changed in July 2024, when he was taken into U.S. custody after arriving at a small airport in New Mexico aboard a private plane with Joaquín Guzmán López, El Chapo’s son. Netflix’s documentary examines what happened and the lingering questions surrounding El Mayo’s capture.

The Story Behind El Mayo’s Downfall

The Fall of El Mayo is directed by Darren Foster and Miguel Fernández Flores and uses interviews, previously unseen archival footage and hundreds of court records from Mexico and the United States to reconstruct the events surrounding Zambada’s downfall.

Netflix says the filmmakers spoke with security agents, politicians, journalists, people connected to the cartel and families affected by the violence surrounding it.

The series consists of three episodes, each running roughly an hour, making it a relatively quick binge for viewers interested in true crime and organized crime documentaries. All three episodes were released simultaneously on Oct. 29.

It’s Already Beating Some Major Netflix Shows

The No. 1 ranking puts The Fall of El Mayo ahead of several established Netflix hits.

As of Oct. 31, The One About Matthew Perry is No. 2 on the U.S. series chart, followed by Nobody Wants This, Love Is Blind and Instadocs: Jane Doe v. Cornell.

The documentary’s quick rise is particularly notable because it only debuted Thursday. By Saturday, it had already climbed to the top of Netflix’s U.S. series rankings.

For anyone looking for something new to watch this weekend, The Fall of El Mayo is clearly getting plenty of attention, and its three-episode format makes it an easy series to finish in one sitting.

MINNEAPOLIS, MINNESOTA - OCTOBER 25: Jonathan Taylor #28 of the Indianapolis Colts looks on prior to the start of the game against the Minnesota Vikings at U.S. Bank Stadium on October 25, 2026 in Minneapolis, Minnesota. (Photo by David Berding/Getty Images) | Getty Images

According to NFL Insider Tom Pelissero in ‘The Sweep,’ Indianapolis Colts star running back Jonathan Taylor is available via trade, but it would take a significant draft capital haul for the franchise to part with its last remaining key veteran pillar:

Colts RB Jonathan Taylor: How expensive would it be to get Taylor out of Indianapolis? The Colts have already been offered a 2027 second-round draft pick plus an additional pick for Taylor—and they immediately rejected it. Even after trading Nelson and Buckner, the Colts aren’t in fire sale mode. Taylor is only 27, he is the face of the franchise, and he just signed a two-year, $44 million contract extension in August. The Colts won’t deal him for anything other than a premium haul—and rightfully so. The Colts haven’t told other teams exactly what they want for Taylor, but it makes sense they’d want something close to deal the Panthers got when they traded Christian McCaffrey to San Francisco in 2022 in exchange for 2023 second-, third-, and fourth-round picks plus a 2024 fifth-rounder. Taylor is due about $4.866 million for the rest of this season, including active roster bonuses. His $15 million base salary for 2027 becomes fully guaranteed five days after the Super Bowl.

The Colts reportedly were quick to turn down overtures of a second round pick, as Indianapolis appears to be holding out hope for at least a first round pick to part with their star workhorse during a busy week for the franchise, which already saw the Horseshoe trade fellow veterans Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner.

It’s already been reported that Colts team owner and CEO Carlie Irsay-Gordon, who met with Taylor earlier this week, considers him potentially a major piece of the ongoing rebuild that was recently jumpstarted after such a disappointing start to Indianapolis’ season (at 2-5), that also saw longtime general manager Chris Ballard, the NFL executive who traded for and drafted Taylor back in the 2nd round of the 2020 NFL Draft, recently fired.

Per ESPN’s Stephen Holder and longtime CBS 4/FOX 59’s Mike Chappell, while the Colts are currently listening on offers for Taylor from prospective trade suitors, Indianapolis isn’t actively shopping the franchise’s 2nd all-time career rushing leader (8,185) and all-time rushing touchdowns leader (77) either:

DC’s Strangest New Show Just Wrapped Filming

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

The DC Universe is getting weird.

The People v. Gorilla Grodd, the bizarre Superman spinoff starring Skyler Gisondo as Jimmy Olsen, has officially wrapped filming and is now moving into post-production.

Collider reported that Gisondo confirmed production had finished, while also teasing that the series takes an approach to the DC Universe that audiences haven’t seen before.

That’s fitting for a show whose premise sounds unlike anything else currently coming out of DC.

Jimmy Olsen Is Investigating Gorilla Grodd

The eight-episode HBO Max comedy is structured like a true-crime documentary, with Jimmy Olsen investigating one of Metropolis’ strangest murder cases.

Gorilla Grodd, the superintelligent ape, has been convicted of murdering his father, the King of Gorilla City. Jimmy isn’t convinced the case is that simple, so he reopens the investigation to determine whether Grodd was actually responsible.

The series comes from Tony Yacenda and Dan Perrault, the creators behind American Vandal, which should give viewers a pretty good idea of the mockumentary style they’re going for. Yacenda is directing all eight episodes.

Gisondo is returning as Jimmy after playing the character in James Gunn’s Superman. Jimmy Tatro will play Gorilla Grodd, while Beck Bennett, Mikaela Hoover and Wendell Pierce are also returning as Steve Lombard, Cat Grant and Perry White, respectively.

It Could Be Very Different From Superman

That’s part of what makes the project so interesting.

Rather than simply giving Jimmy Olsen another traditional superhero adventure, The People v. Gorilla Grodd is being presented as a documentary that exists within the DC Universe itself. The filmmakers have described it as something that should feel like an actual Daily Planet production made for the people of Metropolis.

Gisondo previously told Collider that the mockumentary format was one of the things that attracted him to the project. He said he had never seen anything quite like a true-crime mockumentary set inside a superhero world.

And now that filming is finished, DC fans are one step closer to seeing whether that unusual idea actually works.

When Will The People v. Gorilla Grodd Be Released?

There’s still no exact premiere date for the series.

HBO Max ordered the show straight to series in August, with eight half-hour episodes planned. James Gunn and Peter Safran are executive producers alongside Yacenda and Perrault.

For now, The People v. Gorilla Grodd is simply another sign that Gunn’s DC Universe isn’t afraid to take some very unexpected swings.

A true-crime comedy about Jimmy Olsen investigating whether a superpowered gorilla murdered his own father certainly qualifies.
