Reed Sheppard gets paid… well, kind of

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Yesterday afternoon, Adam Spolane reported that the Houston Rockets had picked up Reed Sheppard’s fourth-year option. This means for the 2027-2028 season, Sheppard will be making just above $14 million before he is eligible for a rookie contract extension. It could be seen as an odd move for the Rockets, who had some questions about where Sheppard would fit into the rotation throughout this season, but it does seem like they want to keep him around for at least this year and next year before potentially extending him in free agency after the 2027 season.

Sheppard is the 10th first-round pick the Rockets have selected from 2021-24. He is the sixth of 10 players to have his fourth-year option picked up by the team. The previous five, Jalen Green, Alperen Sengun, Jabari Smith Jr., Tari Eason and Amen Thompson, all inked contract extensions with Houston. After a rough debut year, Sheppard has become a fixture in the Rockets’ rotation. He moved into being Fred VanVleet’s backup at point guard from sliding completely out of the rotation. He also played three games for the Rockets’ G League team in Rio Grande Valley in January 2025 to get more playing time.

The Rockets feel Sheppard still can play point guard at an NBA level, but with VanVleet returning and Marcus Smart joining the team as a free agent, he’s been playing largely off the ball through four games this season.

“He’s a guy that has to get better at pick and roll,” Rockets head coach Ime Udoka stated on media day. “He’s played off-ball his whole life, and you know he’s really good at it.

Numbers-wise, Sheppard does seem to have the ability to contribute as a scorer, playmaker and at times on the perimeter defensively, but he does need to figure out a way to be able to be a lot more efficient with his shooting from the field and from three.

Sheppard is definitely capable of improving his efficiency this year if he can play more off-ball and take advantage of playmakers like Fred VanVleet and Alperen Sengun, who can both set up Sheppard with great catch-and-shoot situations to create more separation from the defense when coming off screens and cuts. Sheppard might also develop as a shooter, just by acquiring more confidence over the season.

It should also be noted that Sheppard has had some injuries this season, including a left knee issue that helped him out against the San Antonio Spurs on October 23, and a back injury that kept him out of the lineup against the Dallas Mavericks.

The injuries Sheppard has suffered so far this year don’t appear to be severe, but they are crucial to keep in mind in case it is impacting his play on the court when he is available.

It is clear that the front office has belief in Reed Sheppard, and time will tell to see how he develops this season and beyond.

A Pristine 1950s John Lautner ‘Time Capsule’ Just Listed for $2.1M—and It’s Had Only One Owner

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Referred to as the Rosemary and Joseph Baldwin Residence, a John Lautner-designed home seeks a new steward in the trendy Los Angeles neighborhood of Silver Lake.

But what’s even more rare is that this three-bedroom, two-bath property spanning 1,679 square feet has had just one owner. With very few updates, it’s a veritable time capsule.

Tucked into a wooded, hillside setting, Lautner balanced the home on posts anchored by shear walls. This creates a floating effect, made even more dramatic with floor-to-ceiling glass and a wraparound deck. That the home is seamlessly integrated into nature, including redwood-plank siding and skylights that coax in nature light, are two other marvels.

“The house is completely original, minus a tree that was growing through the teak deck in the backyard and that needed to be removed,” says Louis Magnano of Modern California House, who is handling the $2,099,999 listing. “It’s about as original as you can get in 2026.”

John Lautner designed the house by balancing it on posts, so it appears like it's floating in the trees. (Cameron Carothers) The living room's fireplace is original to the home. (Cameron Carothers) Adjacent to the home's courtyard is this dining nook. (Cameron Carothers)

This includes the entire kitchen as well as lighting fixtures, mahogany paneling, and terrazzo and parquet flooring. As well, the fireplace is original, and all of the windows, too. Each bedroom’s built-ins are also intact. The living room’s inverted-pyramid ceiling and triangular clerestory windows are also striking.

“It’s completely original, which is very rare for these types of homes in L.A.,” says Magnano.

“They get traded (and) things are done to them when they shouldn’t be. This house doesn’t have that. Whoever is going to purchase this home and be the next steward will need to restore and bring it up to today’s standards but while maintaining the original quality of the home. The sellers want to see it restored.”

This could mean updating the systems, for starters.

How often does a Lautner-designed home hop onto the market, though?

“They have been coming up quite frequently for sale in the last few years,” says Magnano, “but there’s so few and far between, maybe it’s just a special time in the marketplace.”

In 1955, Lautner—a Taliesin Fellow under Frank Lloyd Wright during the 1930s—was commissioned by commercial artist James Baldwin and his wife Rosemary to create their family home, utilizing a 7,491-square-foot lot. Rosemary’s sister introduced them to Lautner.

Lautner’s residence (Silvertop) is on the same street as the Baldwin home.

“Silver Lake is really known for a lot of its architectural history, not only by John Lautner, but Richard Neutra, Rudolph Schinder, to name a few starchitects,” says Magnano.

Lautner would later design three other Southern California homes that put him on the map: The Chemosphere (1960), The Sheats-Goldstein Residence (1961-1963) and The Elrod Residence (1968).

“The Baldwin Residence marks a pivotal moment in that career: its siting, spatial progression, and detail recall Wright, even as they reveal Lautner's own emerging voice,” reads the listing, adding that this is “an authentic piece of Los Angeles architectural history and a rare stewardship opportunity that may not come again.”

Within many rooms, including this bedroom, are original built-ins. (Cameron Carothers) Like the rest of the home, the kitchen has been preserved and remains in its original state. (Cameron Carothers) Throughout the home, including in this bath, there's a conscious effort to invite natural light in. (Cameron Carothers)

In addition to the striking interior design, another wow-worthy feature are views of the Hollywood Hills and San Gabriel Mountains.

What will also likely attract buyers is the location. As a haven for artists, it’s similar to the type of community that drew the Baldwins there back in the 1950s.

“Silver Lake has a very rich artist community and it’s vibrant, with young people, and there’s new cafes and a lot of nightlife happening in the area,” says Magnano. “There’s a lot of development happening in Silver Lake and a big restoration project taking place in the reservoir that the city of Los Angeles is getting ready to break ground on soon, to beautify the area.”

The ideal buyer, he says, is “somebody who understands the significance of these historical properties, who wants to restore them and honor them and keep them in the original glory that they all are. It was a family home for the Baldwins. They had three children. It could be a family home (again) but it could be somebody’s second home or ‘home away from home.’”

Chainlink Unveils 24/7 Pricing for Tokenized Assets as Aave Adopts Feeds

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

TL;DR: The service extends a prior 24/5 equity-data product into weekends and holidays. An oracle feed is not a guarantee of a redeemable cash-market price, and an off-hours derivative reference can diverge from the next opening auction.

Chainlink introduced its 24/7 data service on October 29 to supply reference prices for equities, commodities and other real-world assets beyond conventional exchange hours. It identified lending protocol Aave as the launch partner for using the feeds to value eligible collateral.

The announcement and the numbers

The most concrete starting point is the dated disclosure from Chainlink. It offers a specific basis for assessing Chainlink’s round-the-clock real-world asset price feeds without turning the story into unsupported price speculation.

The service extends a prior 24/5 equity-data product into weekends and holidays. Chainlink said off-hours references combine information from several perpetual-futures venues, with stale data filtering and outlier controls, rather than treating a closed stock exchange as if it were still publishing live prices.

Announcements in crypto often arrive before the data needed to price their longer-term significance. A sensible reading separates what has already been delivered from what still needs commercial adoption, technical validation or regulatory clearance.

Why the development matters

The question for market participants is what Chainlink’s round-the-clock real-world asset price feeds establishes today and what remains uncertain. An immediate reaction is not the same as evidence of a durable economic outcome.

Continuous reference data can support longer trading sessions, lending and prediction markets. The larger question is whether financial risk engines can make defensible decisions on Saturday using prices derived from instruments whose liquidity and economics differ from the underlying asset.

A pilot, framework or technical partnership should not be conflated with a live secondary market. Investors need to know whether assets have actually been issued, whether redemption is possible and where responsibility sits if records disagree.

A durable signal would be consistent follow-through: actual usage, transparent reporting and a credible explanation of risk. Short-term token-price moves can be driven by macroeconomic conditions or leverage and may say little about whether the product or initiative succeeds over months and years.

Risks the headline does not resolve

None of this removes the need for independent assessment. The published evidence identifies a development, while its risk and long-term economics are still open to examination.

A tokenized share can trade or support a loan when the underlying cash market is closed, creating a mismatch between token liquidity and reference pricing. Oracles deliver data to smart contracts, but the usefulness of a quote depends on its source quality, freshness and suitability for liquidation decisions.

An oracle feed is not a guarantee of a redeemable cash-market price, and an off-hours derivative reference can diverge from the next opening auction. Protocols need conservative collateral factors, circuit breakers and governance around unusual market conditions.

Representing an asset on a blockchain is the relatively straightforward part of tokenization. Making that representation useful requires legal rights, reliable valuation, restrictions on eligible holders, custody and a clear process for settlement and corporate actions.

The milestones to watch

The immediate checklist is relatively straightforward: look for verifiable implementation details and avoid interpreting a stated target as an outcome that has already occurred.

Watch which assets gain coverage, how Aave manages liquidations outside exchange hours, and whether data stays robust during thin liquidity. Sustainable usage is more important than a claim that a particular market is now technically available every hour.

From a market perspective, the first task is to separate the scale of the announcement from the scale of economic activity that has actually occurred. A company may report a large addressable market, an institutional name or a future capability, but those are not equivalent to settled transactions or recurring fee income. This is why the primary disclosure is a better starting point than a trading narrative attached to it.

Investors also need to separate changes in infrastructure from changes in token demand. Revenue may accrue to a company, a protocol treasury or a service provider without necessarily creating a one-for-one benefit for holders of a related token. Market impact depends on adoption, competition and the design of whatever fees or incentives are involved.
